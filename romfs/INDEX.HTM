Hello from a SNES

This page came off cartridge ROM, through a 65816 at 3.58 MHz.

The console runs its own TCP/IP stack. The packets carrying this page were composed here — IP headers, TCP sequence numbers, checksums and all — by a 65816. They travel as raw datagrams over what will be a controller-port cable to a box doing nothing cleverer than address translation, which is why the address you typed is not the console's own. The HTTP was composed here too, by httpd.

It serves one connection at a time at about 615 bytes per second. Your browser opened six connections; five of them are waiting.
