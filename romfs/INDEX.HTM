Hello from a SNES

This page came off cartridge ROM, through a 65816 at 3.58 MHz.

The console has no TCP/IP stack. It speaks a 64-byte framed protocol over what will be a controller-port cable, and a gateway on the other side turns that into sockets. The HTTP you are reading was composed here, on the SNES, by httpd.

It serves one connection at a time at about 615 bytes per second. Your browser opened six connections; five of them are waiting.
